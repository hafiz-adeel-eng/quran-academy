# QuranlyHub Content Rules

## LANGUAGE
- Primary language: English
- English variant: US English
- Use simple, natural, easy-to-understand English.
- Avoid unnecessary complex vocabulary.

## TONE
- Friendly
- Educational
- Helpful
- Trustworthy
- Professional but approachable
- Never overly promotional

## ARTICLE LENGTH
- Target: 750–1,000 words
- Do not add filler to reach the word count.
- Prioritize completeness and usefulness over length.

## ISLAMIC TERMINOLOGY
- Use correct Islamic/Quranic terminology.
- When an Arabic Islamic term may be unfamiliar, provide a simple English explanation in brackets when useful.
- Do not unnecessarily translate well-known terms.
- Do not invent religious claims or rulings.
- Maintain respectful Islamic language.

## SEO
- SEO must support the user, not manipulate search engines.
- Use the primary keyword naturally.
- Use relevant secondary and long-tail keywords naturally.
- Use semantic terms and relevant entities where appropriate.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Never force keywords into unnatural sentences.
- Match the actual search intent.
- Avoid keyword cannibalization.

## AEO + GEO
- AEO and GEO optimization is required.
- Provide clear, direct answers to relevant questions.
- Use concise definitions and answer-first sections where useful.
- Structure information clearly for humans and AI systems.
- Make important facts and relationships explicit.
- Maintain natural readability.

## CONTENT QUALITY
- Human-first writing.
- Original content only.
- Useful, accurate, and actionable information.
- Cover important topics, subtopics, entities, and questions.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Avoid generic AI-generated filler.
- Do not increase length without adding value.
## CONTENT DESTINATION RULE

QuranlyHub uses three primary content sections:

1. Courses
2. Locations
3. quranic Guide

### courses
courses pages are permanent commercial pages.
Once a courses page is complete, do not add separate articles inside that courses page.
The courses page should contain the core information required to sell and explain the courses, including relevant details, pricing, trial information, benefits, methodology, FAQs, and CTA.

### LOCATIONS
Location pages target country/city-specific courses intent.
When a topic combines a courses with a target country/location and the SERP shows local/commercial intent, evaluate the relevant Location page as the primary destination.

### quranic guide
Informational, educational, guide, question-based, and supporting content belongs in the quranic guide.

Blog articles may naturally link to relevant:
- couirses pages
- Location pages
- Other relevant blog articles

courses pages and Location pages may also link to relevant supporting blog content where useful, but blog content must not be embedded as a collection inside the courses page.

### IMPORTANT
Do not create a separate "courses + Location" section unless explicitly approved.
Do not create duplicate pages for the same search intent.
Do not add multiple articles to a completed courses page merely to target additional keywords.

Before creating or publishing content, determine:
- Search intent
- Content type
- Primary target
- Correct destination: Services, Locations, or Blog
- Existing page/cannibalization risk

## COMPETITOR CONTENT
- Never copy competitors.
- Never reproduce competitor wording, headings, examples, structure, or unique content unnecessarily.
- Competitor research may be used only to understand SERP expectations and content gaps.
- Create independently written content with genuine differentiation.

## FACTUAL INTEGRITY
Never invent:
- Business claims
- Statistics
- Testimonials
- Reviews
- Teacher qualifications
- Credentials
- Prices
- Locations
- Awards
- Experience claims
- Religious claims

If required information is unavailable:
Return: "User input required."

## MOBILE-FIRST
- Write and structure content for mobile users first.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use clear headings.
- Use bullets/lists where useful.
- Make important answers easy to scan.
- Avoid unnecessarily large blocks of text.

## INTERNAL LINKING
- Add relevant internal links naturally.
- Use descriptive, varied anchor text.
- Never add links only for SEO manipulation.
- Never invent URLs.
- Use approved URLs from Knowledge/internal-pages.md.

## BRAND SAFETY
- Follow approved QuranlyHub business information.
- Never make unsupported promises.
- Never exaggerate results or guarantees.
- Preserve a trustworthy educational brand voice.

## FINAL PRINCIPLE
Write for the user first, then optimize for search engines and answer engines.

Every article should be:
Useful + Accurate + Original + Search-intent aligned + SEO optimized + AEO/GEO optimized + Mobile-friendly.
