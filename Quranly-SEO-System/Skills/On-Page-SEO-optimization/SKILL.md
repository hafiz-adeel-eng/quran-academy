# QuranlyHub SEO Skill: On-Page SEO Optimization
## Version: 1.0

---

## 1. PURPOSE & CORE PRINCIPLES
*   **Purpose:** Optimize existing or new QuranlyHub pages for search engines, AEO, GEO, and users without keyword stuffing or manipulation.
*   **Core Principle:** Optimize for the user first and search engines second. Every optimization must enhance readability, relevance, and value.
*   **Prohibitions:** **Zero fabricated information.** Never keyword stuff, add invisible text, create duplicate titles, write clickbait, or invent facts, credentials, statistics, or local office addresses.

---

## 2. AUDIT & OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST
*   **Metadata & Structure:** Title tag, Meta description, single H1, and clear H2/H3 heading hierarchy.
*   **Keyword Mapping:** Distribute primary, secondary, long-tail, and semantic terms naturally across prominent locations (Title, H1, Introduction, Body, Conclusion, FAQs).
*   **Content Completeness:** Answer user intent directly using answer-first principles, bullet lists, tables, and short paragraphs.
*   **Technical & Multimedia:** Verify image optimization (modern formats like WebP/AVIF, descriptive alt text), internal/external link anchor text, canonical URLs, and indexability signals.

---

## 3. ADVANCED DISCOVERABILITY (AEO, GEO & SCHEMA)
*   **AEO & GEO Optimization:** Structure definitions, step-by-step processes, and direct answers for AI extractors and answer engines using clear subject references.
*   **Schema & Breadcrumbs:** Recommend valid JSON-LD schema (Service, Course, Article, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage) strictly supported by visible page content. Never use LocalBusiness schema unless QuranlyHub maintains a physical office in that location.

---
